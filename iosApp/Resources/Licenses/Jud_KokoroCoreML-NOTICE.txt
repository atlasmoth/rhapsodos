Rhapsodos retains only the English phonemizer and its required dictionaries. Acoustic models, DSP, tokenizer and download APIs are removed. Plain PDF text is processed literally; pronunciation-markup interpretation is removed to preserve source contents.
Plain PDF text is not interpreted as pronunciation markup. Unsupported BART graphemes fail explicitly rather than being dropped.
